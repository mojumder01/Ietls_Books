Introduction:

I like to go many different places depending on my mood. However, I’m

going to tell you a special place which is the most attractive in my country. I

will explain where it is and how I went there. I will try and describe what it

looks like and explain why I like the location so much.

a. Where is this place?

I have several amazing places where I intend to visit such as Sylhet, Khulna,

Cox’sBazar and so on. Among them, Cox’sbazar which has the

longest sea beach in the world, is one of the most attractive

places in my eyes or at least as good as others. Cox’sbazar is the southern

part of Bangladesh. It’s 200 kilometers away from the main division.

one of the most attractive places =

C. When and how did you go there?

- Actually, I went there on September 2nd, 2021 with

some of my colleagues. To go there, I required to purchase

advanced air ticket prior to the departure, the air fare was

4,300 taka which was really reasonable. But mostly I go there

by bus. The bus journey takes around 6 to 7 hours and the

air journey takes only 40 to 50 minutes.

Page 10 / 14

B. Why do you/did you prefer this place?

- I want to know why you are here.

- Now the question is why I do prefer this place. Firstly,

cox’sbazar is not only famous for the sea beach but also

renowned for it its apex hills. The sound of sea waves charms

me a lot. Secondly, the sea food is really delicious and you

know Sea food contains vitamin D. Thirdly, the whole

environment is so cool which helps me to reduce anxiety and

stress. Moreover, the people of this area are really easy-

going, hospitable and generous.

Law

Describe a traffic rule or Law you know about…

You should say:

a. What is it?

b. How did you know about it?

Page 11 / 14

c. Whether you follow this rule or not?

Introduction:

Laws are always made for human benefits. I am neither a law

expert nor I have sufficient knowledge regarding this. But I like to

believe for sure that each and every country in the world has its

own set of traffic laws to maintain the order and discipline on

their roads. From my point of view, traffic rule is one of the

most crucial/significant laws in a country or at least as good

as others.

a. What is it?

The law, I am talking about, is a law/ that allows the traffic

authority to punish a drunk driver/ by suspending his driving

license for a long period of time and then putting him into

prison for 2 years. In addition to these punishments, the

authority is also allowed to issue a fine of hefty amount of

money.

b. How did you know about it?

Now I am going to tell how I knew about this special law. The

answer is that I had to learn this law in order to get a driving

license after passing the written test together with a road

test.

Page 12 / 14

c. Whether you follow this rule or not?

- I always abide by all the laws. But to be honest, very few

times I drive over the speed limit when roads are almost

empty. I am afraid I might be fined for this. It’s really difficult

to accept strict laws for anyone when fines or punishments

are imposed on himself or herself.

Imaginary something:

As far as I could vividly remember, some days ago, my neighbor

Mr. Abdullah who is very naïve person was fined 50,000 taka

because of expiring his driving license only for 2 days. Since it was

the time of anti-government strike, he could not renew his

license. The amount was hefty and the government should

balance between penalty and occurrence albeit is a debatable

issue.

Albeit = although = যমেও
