Describe a piece of software that you use often

You should say:

•What it is

•What you use it for

•How you got to know about it

•And explain why you use it

Introduction

To be perfectly honest, I’m not entirely sure if the software that I’m going to talk about is

classed as a piece of software or an application because I’m not an IT geek, but I know it’s not

hardware so I’m guessing it is software, anyway I’ve decided to talk about Microsoft Office

which is one of the most useful software among many others or at least as good as others.

For as long as I can remember, I’ve been using a version of Office and, in a nutshell*, it is

indispensable/obligatory. When I got my first PC, many years ago, it came with an Office

package and I’ve been utilizing it ever since. As you’re probably aware, there are various

components such as word, excel and PowerPoint and the others have slipped my mind*, but

actually I don’t use any of the other bits of it.

Like I said, I mainly use three parts of Office – word for typing documents, excel for producing

spreadsheets and PowerPoint for presentations. I’ve been making use of it for years, but sadly

I’m no expert and really, I should go on a course to become more proficient. I’m just so snowed

under with work that I struggle to get my work done so I don’t have time to learn how to use it

better. I know I should because if I became more IT/computer literate, then I would probably

be able to get my work done more quickly.

With word and PowerPoint, I’m an average user, but I’m pretty hopeless when it comes to

excel. I wish I could use it better so I really should find time or make time to do a course. It

would save me time in the long run because I waste hours trying to figure out how to do stuff

in it. Mind you, despite being a satisfactory user of it at best, I couldn’t live without it.

Describe a traffic rule or law you know about

You should say:

• What is it?

• How do you know about this law?

• Whether you follow this rule/law or not

Introduction:

Laws are always made for human benefits. I am neither a law expert nor I have sufficient

knowledge regarding this traffic rules. But I like to believe for sure that each and every

country in this world has its own set of traffic laws to maintain order and discipline on

their road. From my point of view, traffic rule is one of the most significant laws in a

country or at least as good as others.

What is it?

The law, I am talking about, is a law that allows the traffic authority of my country to

punish a drunk driver by suspending his driving license for a long period of time and then

putting him into prison for 2 years. In addition to these punishments, the authority is also

allowed to fine a hefty amount of money which I can’t remember exactly.

How do you know about this law?

Now the question is how I knew about this special law. The answer is that I had to learn

this law in order to get a driving license after passing the written test together with a road

test.

Whether you follow this rule/law or not

I always abide by all the laws. But to be honest, very few times I drive over the speed

limit when roads are almost empty. I am afraid I might be fined for this. It’s really

difficult to accept strict laws when fines or punishments are imposed on himself or

herself.

Imaginary something:

As far as I could vividly remember, some days ago, my neighbor Mr. Abdullah who is

very naïve person was fined 7000 tk just because of expiring his driving license only for

2 days. Since it was the time of Covid-19, he could not renew his license.

Describe a happy experience you have had

• Where you were

• When it happened

• Who you were with

• And explain why you felt happy

When I read this topic, the first thing that came to mind it was a party

that was organized for my 16 birthdays by my parents. It was a surprise party so I didn’t

know anything about it.

To be honest, I actually thought that my friends had forgotten my birthday because no one

was talking about it or asking me what I wanted to do.

When I brought it up, everyone dismissed it by saying ‘sorry, I’m a bit busy at the moment so

can we do something next month to celebrate it?’ This made me incredibly depressed that no

one could be bothered to spend time with me on my special day. My parents were aware of my

distress and suggested that we go out for a slap-up meal instead. What I didn’t know was that

they were secretly planning this surprise party for me.

On the day of my birthday, I got a few text messages from close friends wishing me a happy

birthday. Even though the day was good because I went out shopping with my parents and

siblings and I got a brand-new iPad, I felt a bit down because I thought that my friends were

ignoring me. At 5pm, we went to the restaurant and as I walked through the doors, I heard

‘surprise’ and was gob smacked to see all of my friends and family there.

I was astounded that my friends and parents had managed to organize this event and no one

had breathed a word to me. I was thrilled to bits that all the people who are special to me were

in that room. The food was delicious and then there was a disco so we danced until the early

hours. I’m not sure any birthday can top that one and I’ll certainly never forget it.

Describe a place where you visited recently

a. Where is this place?

b. Why do you like this place?

c. When and how did you go there?

Introduction:

I like to go to many different places depending on my mood. Today, however, I am going

to tell you about a special place which is the most attractive place in my country. I will

explain where it is and how I went there. I will try and describe what it looks like and

explain why I like the location so much.

Where is this place?

Well, my upcoming describing tourist place where I had visited recently is cox’sbazar.

From my point of view, this place is one of my most attractive places in my eyes or at least

as good as others. Cox’sbazar is the southern part of Bangladesh. It is 200 kilometers away

from the main division.

Why do you like this place?

Now the question is why I do prefer this place most. Firstly, Cox’sbazar is not only famous

for the sea beach but also renowned for its apex hills. Secondly, the sea food is really

delicious. Thirdly, the people of this city are generous, easy going and hospitable.

Moreover, the whole environment is so cool which helps me to reduce anxiety and stress.

When and how did you go there?

Actually, I went there on 22 September, 2021 with some of my colleagues. To go there, I

required to purchase advance air ticket 2-3 days prior to the departure.

Imaginary something:

As far as I could vividly remember when we went cox’sbazar last time suddenly we found

a one-legged-person while we were enjoying the sea roar in the afternoon. He asked for

food from us then we brought him to a restaurant and fed him as far as he could. He was

so happy that he could not express. Never have I seen such sort of happiest person in my

life.
