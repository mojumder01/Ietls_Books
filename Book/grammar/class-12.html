Tense

1. Present Indefinite Tense

Rule: কক্ায়িা ক্াজ দিেদিত, অভ্যােেত বা েববজিীি েতয কবাঝায়ত Present Indefinite Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + verb (present form) + object

Interrogative Structure: Do/Does + subject + verb (base form) + object?

Example:

I always wake up early in the morning because maintaining discipline is the key to achieving success.

2. Past Indefinite Tense

Rule: অতীয়ত দির্িিষ্ট েিয়ে েম্পন্ন িওো ক্াজ কবাঝায়ত Past Indefinite Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + verb (past form) + object

Interrogative Structure: Did + subject + verb (base form) + object?

Example:

I completed my undergraduate thesis last year, which significantly enhanced my research skills.

3. Future Indefinite Tense

Rule: ভ্দবষ্যয়ত কক্ায়িা ক্াজ েম্পন্ন িয়ব কবাঝায়ত Future Indefinite Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + shall/will + verb (base form) + object

Interrogative Structure: Shall/Will + subject + verb (base form) + object ?

Example:

I will pursue higher education abroad to broaden my career opportunities.

4. Present Continuous Tense

Rule: বতবিায়ি চলিাি ক্াজ কবাঝায়ত Present Continuous Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + am/is/are + verb (ing) + object

Interrogative Structure: Am/Is/Are + subject + verb (ing) + object ?

Example:

At the moment, many students are preparing intensively for their IELTS examination.

5. Past Continuous Tense

Rule: অতীয়ত কক্ায়িা েিে চলিাি ক্াজ কবাঝায়ত Past Continuous Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + was/were + verb (ing) + object

Interrogative Structure: Was/Were + subject + verb (ing) + object ?

Example:

I was studying in the library when the fire alarm suddenly rang.

6. Future Continuous Tense

Rule: ভ্দবষ্যয়তর দির্িিষ্ট েিয়ে ক্াজ চলিাি থাক্য়ব কবাঝায়ত Future Continuous Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + shall/will + be + verb (ing) + object

Interrogative Structure: Shall/Will + subject + be + verb (ing) + object ?

Example:

By this time next week, I will be attending an international conference in Singapore.

7. Present Perfect Tense

Rule: অতীয়ত শুরু িওো এবং এর প্রভ্াব বতবিায়ি রয়েয়ে এিি ক্াজ কবাঝায়ত Present Perfect Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + has/have + past participle + object

Interrogative Structure: Has/Have + subject + past participle + object ?

Example:

I have completed several online courses that enhanced my critical thinking skills.

8. Past Perfect Tense

Rule: অতীয়ত এক্টি ক্াজ কেষ্ িওোর আয়ে আয়রক্টি ক্াজ ঘয়িয়ে কবাঝায়ত Past Perfect Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + had + past participle + object

Interrogative Structure: Had + subject + past participle + object ?

Example:

By the time the seminar started, I had already prepared my presentation slides.

9. Future Perfect Tense

Rule: ভ্দবষ্যয়তর দির্িিষ্ট েিয়ের িয়যয ক্াজ েম্পন্ন িয়ে যায়ব কবাঝায়ত Future Perfect Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + shall/will + have + past participle + object

Interrogative Structure: Shall/Will + subject + have + past participle + object ?

Example:

By the end of this year, I will have achieved my target IELTS score.

10. Present Perfect Continuous Tense

Rule: অতীয়ত শুরু িয়ে এখয়িা চলিাি ক্াজ কবাঝায়ত Present Perfect Continuous Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + has/have + been + verb (ing) + object + since/for + time

Interrogative Structure: Has/Have + subject + been + verb (ing) + object ?

Example:

I have been preparing for the IELTS examination for the last six months.

11. Past Perfect Continuous Tense

Rule: অতীয়ত এক্টি দির্িিষ্ট েিে পযবন্ত ক্াজ চলদেল কবাঝায়ত Past Perfect Continuous Tense বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Subject + had + been + verb (ing) + object + since/for + time

Interrogative Structure: Had + subject + been + verb (ing) + object ?

Example:

She had been working at the company for five years before she finally resigned.

12. Future Perfect Continuous Tense

13. Use of Cannot but / Cannot help

Rule: Cannot but অথবা Cannot help এর পর verb েবেিে ing form িে।

Structure: Subject + cannot but/cannot help + verb (ing)

Example:

I cannot help appreciating the government’s effort in promoting renewable energy.

14. Use of Before / After

Rule:

•

Past Perfect tense + before + Past Indefinite tense

•

Past Indefinite tense + after + Past Perfect tense

Structure:

Clause 1 (Past Perfect) + before + Clause 2 (Past Indefinite)

OR

Clause 1 (Past Indefinite) + after + Clause 2 (Past Perfect)

Example:

I had completed my research before the supervisor asked for a progress report.

15. Use of As if / As though

Rule:

•

Present Indefinite / Present Perfect tense + as if/as though + Past Indefinite tense

•

Past Indefinite tense + as if/as though + Past Perfect tense

Example:

He speaks as if he knew the solution to every global issue.

16. Use of Since / Because

Rule:

•

Present Perfect / Present Indefinite tense + since + Past Indefinite tense

•

Past Indefinite tense + since + Past Perfect tense

Example:

I have not travelled abroad since I graduated from university.

17. Use of Unless

Rule: Unless িায়ি "যদি িা"। এটি Negative অথব প্রক্াে ক্য়র।

Structure: Unless + condition (Present Indefinite), Future Indefinite result

Example:

Unless we reduce carbon emissions, future generations will face severe climate challenges.

18. Use of Preposition + Verb (ing)

Rule: Preposition এর পয়র েবেিে verb এর ing form িে।

Common Phrases: with a view to, look forward to, get used to

Example:

I am looking forward to studying at a prestigious university abroad.

19. Use of Verb (ing) at the Beginning

Rule: Verb এর ing form দিয়ে বাক্য শুরু ক্রা যাে।

Example:

Travelling broadens our perspective by exposing us to diverse cultures.

20. Past Participle as Adjective

Rule: Noun এর আয়ে verb এর past participle form adjective দিয়েয়ব বযবহৃত িে।

Examples: admitted students, prohibited area, allowed place

Example:

The admitted students were provided with free access to the digital library.

21. Use of Adverbs (Already, Recently, Probably)

Rule: েিে বা েম্ভাবিা কবাঝায়ত already, recently, probably ইতযাদি বযবহৃত িে।

Example:

I will probably migrate to Canada next year to enhance my professional growth.

Noun

1. Use of Uncountable Noun

Rule: Uncountable noun plural িে িা এবং এর আয়ে a/an বয়ে িা।

Structure: Subject + verb + (some/much/a lot of) + uncountable noun

Example:

In modern society, much information is accessible online, but not all of it is reliable.

2. Use of Proper Noun

Rule: Proper noun েবেিে capital letter দিয়ে শুরু িে। োযারণত article কিে িা।

Structure: Subject + verb + Proper Noun

Example:

Shakespeare’s works continue to inspire writers and thinkers worldwide.

3. Use of Collective Noun

Rule: Collective noun োযারণত singular verb কিে, তয়ব েিেযয়ির আলািা কবাঝায়ল plural verb বযবিার িে।

Structure: Subject (Collective Noun) + singular/plural verb

Example:

The team is working towards a sustainable solution, though some members are divided in their

opinions.

4. Use of Abstract Noun

Rule: Abstract noun িৃেযিাি িে, বরং গুণ/ভ্াব কবাঝাে। এগুয়লা uncountable িে।

Structure: Subject + verb + Abstract Noun

Example:

Honesty is the foundation of a transparent government that truly serves its citizens.

5. Use of Material Noun

Rule: Material noun েবেিে singular থায়ক্ এবং োযারণত article কিে িা।

Structure: Subject + verb + Material Noun

Example:

Gold has been considered a stable form of investment for centuries.

6. Use of Plural Noun

Rule: Plural noun-এর আয়ে োযারণত the বযবিার ক্রা িে যদি দির্িিষ্ট দক্েু কবাঝায়িা িে।

Structure: The + plural noun + verb

Example:

The students of this generation are more inclined towards technology-driven learning.

7. Use of Noun as Adjective

Rule: ক্খয়িা ক্খয়িা noun অিয noun-কক্ modify ক্য়র adjective-এর িয়তা ক্াজ ক্য়র।

Structure: Noun (modifier) + Noun

Example:

Climate change policies require global cooperation to be effective.

8. Use of Compound Noun

Rule: িুই বা তয়তাদযক্ noun দিয়ল িতুি অথব প্রক্াে ক্য়র।

Structure: Noun + Noun (compound form)

Example:

Online education has emerged as a powerful alternative to traditional classroom learning.

9. Use of Possessive Noun

Rule: Possessive noun িাদলক্ািা কবাঝায়ত ’s বা s’ বযবিার িে।

Structure: Noun + ’s + object

Example:

The government’s commitment to renewable energy reflects its responsibility to future generations.

10. Use of Gerund as Noun

Rule: Verb + ing ক্খয়িা noun দিয়েয়ব বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Verb(ing) + verb + object

Example:

Reading broadens the human mind and cultivates empathy across cultures.

Pronoun

Rule: Pronoun subject িয়ল verb singular/plural কিয়ল।

Structure: Pronoun + verb

Example: They are addressing global inequality in education.

2. Object Pronoun with Preposition

Rule: Preposition-এর পয়র েবেিে object pronoun িে।

Structure: Preposition + object pronoun

Example: The decision depends on us.

3. Possessive Pronoun with Noun

Rule: Possessive pronoun-এর পয়র noun বয়ে িা।

Structure: Possessive pronoun + verb

Example: The responsibility is ours.

4. Reflexive Pronoun with Subject

Rule: Subject এবং object এক্ই িয়ল reflexive pronoun িে।

Structure: Subject + verb + reflexive pronoun

Example: She prepared herself for the challenging debate.

5. Demonstrative Pronoun with Verb

Rule: Demonstrative pronoun subject িয়ে singular/plural verb কিে।

Structure: Demonstrative pronoun + verb

Example: These are the solutions we urgently need.

6. Relative Pronoun with Clause

Rule: Relative pronoun িুটি clause যুক্ত ক্য়র।

Structure: Noun + relative pronoun + clause

Example: The leader who inspires others can transform a nation.

7. Interrogative Pronoun with Question

Rule: Interrogative pronoun প্রয়ে বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Interrogative pronoun + auxiliary verb + subject

Example: What is the government doing to reduce unemployment?

8. Indefinite Pronoun with Singular Verb

Rule: Indefinite pronoun (everyone, someone, nobody) singular verb কিে।

Structure: Indefinite pronoun + singular verb

Example: Everyone has a role to play in combating climate change.

9. Reciprocal Pronoun with Plural Subject

Rule: Reciprocal pronoun plural subject-এর োয়থ বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: Plural subject + verb + reciprocal pronoun

Example: Nations must support one another during global crises.

10. Pronoun–Antecedent Agreement

Rule: Pronoun েবেিে antecedent-এর number/gender এর োয়থ কিয়ল।

Structure: Noun (antecedent) + matching pronoun

Example: A student should submit his or her assignment on time.

Adverb

1. Adverb of Manner with Verb

Explanation: Adverb of manner বয়ল ক্াজ কীভাবে িয়েয়ে। এিা োযারণত verb-এর পয়র বয়ে।

Structure: Subject + verb + adverb (manner)

Example:

The students responded positively to the new teaching method.

2. Adverb of Frequency with Main Verb

Explanation: Frequency adverb (always, often, usually, never) main verb-এর আবে বয়ে।

Structure: Subject + frequency adverb + main verb

Example:

People often underestimate the impact of social media on mental health.

3. Adverb of Frequency with “Be” Verb

Explanation: যদি verb = am/is/are/was/were িে, তািয়ল adverb verb-এর পবে বয়ে।

Structure: Subject + be verb + adverb (frequency)

Example:

He is always punctual in his professional commitments.

4. Adverb of Time at Beginning/End

Explanation: Time কবাঝায়ত বযবহৃত adverb (today, yesterday, in 2025, nowadays) োযারণত বায়ক্যর শুরুয়ত বা

কেয়ষ্ বয়ে।

Structure: Adverb of time + subject + verb

Example:

Nowadays, young people rely heavily on digital platforms for learning.

5. Adverb of Place with Verb

Explanation: Place adverb (here, there, everywhere, abroad) োযারণত verb-এর পয়র বয়ে।

Structure: Subject + verb + adverb (place)

Example:

The government should invest more where public facilities are lacking.

6. Adverb of Degree with Adjective/Adverb

Explanation: Degree adverb (very, extremely, too, quite, rather) adjective বা adverb-কক্ modify ক্য়র।

Structure: Subject + verb + adverb of degree + adjective/adverb

Example:

The results are extremely significant for future policymaking.

7. Adverb of Certainty with Sentence

Explanation: Certainty adverb (certainly, definitely, surely, probably) োযারণত subject-এর আয়ে বা পয়র

বয়ে, পুয়রা বাক্যয়ক্ modify ক্য়র।

Structure: Adverb of certainty + subject + verb

Example:

Certainly, technology will continue to transform global communication.

8. Inversion with Negative Adverb

Explanation: Negative adverb (never, rarely, seldom, hardly) দিয়ে বাক্য শুরু িয়ল word order বিয়ল যাে

(inversion িে)।

Structure: Negative adverb + auxiliary verb + subject

Example:

Never have global challenges demanded such urgent international cooperation.

9. Relative Adverb with Clause

Explanation: Relative adverb (when, where, why) িুটি clause যুক্ত ক্য়র। Noun/idea-এর োয়থ বাড়দত তথয কযাে

ক্য়র।

Structure: Clause + relative adverb + clause

Example:

The day when renewable energy overtakes fossil fuel will mark a turning point in history.

10. Comparative/Superlative Adverb

Explanation: Adverb-এর comparison ক্রয়ত িয়ল -er/more (comparative) বা -est/most (superlative) বযবিার

িে।

Structure: Subject + verb + comparative/superlative adverb

Example:

Modern students learn more effectively through interactive methods than traditional lectures.

Article

1. Sound-Based Rule

Rule: A/An বযবহৃত িয়ব েয়ের প্রথি উচ্চারণ অিুোয়র, অক্ষর িা।

Structure: A/An + singular countable noun

Example: An hour of daily reading can improve concentration significantly.

2. First Mention vs Later Mention

Rule: প্রথিবার উয়েখ ক্রয়ল A/An, আবার উয়েখ ক্রয়ল The।

Structure: A/An + noun → later The + same noun

Example: A new metro line was opened last year. The line has reduced traffic congestion.

3. General vs Specific

Rule: োযারণভ্ায়ব কবাঝায়ল article িাই (Ø), দির্িিষ্ট িয়ল The।

Structure: Ø + abstract/common noun (general) / The + noun (specific)

Example: Technology has reshaped society, but the technology used in Japan is world-leading.

4. Countable vs Uncountable

Rule: A/An কক্বল singular countable noun-এর আয়ে বয়ে। Uncountable noun-এ বয়ে িা।

Structure: A/An + singular countable noun / Ø + uncountable noun

Example: Pollution is a global concern, but a type of pollution often ignored is noise pollution.

5. Professions

Rule: কপোর আয়ে েবেিে A/An বযবহৃত িে (singular countable)।

Structure: A/An + profession

Example: She aspires to be an engineer who designs sustainable infrastructure.

6. Fixed Rates & Frequencies

Rule: প্রদত/িাি কবাঝায়ত a/an বযবহৃত িে।

Structure: A/An + unit (time/price/measure)

Example: The train travels at 150 kilometres an hour.

7. Abstract Nouns

Rule: Abstract noun োযারণভ্ায়ব কবাঝায়ল Ø, দির্িিষ্ট িয়ল The।

Structure: Ø + abstract noun (general) / The + abstract noun (specific)

Example: Education is essential for growth, but the education system in rural areas is underfunded.

8. Meals

Rule: খাবায়রর িাি োযারণ িয়ল Ø, দির্িিষ্ট িয়ল The।

Structure: Ø + meal / The + meal (specific)

Example: We usually have lunch at noon, but the lunch served yesterday was extraordinary.

Rule:

10. “A Few” vs “Few”

Rule: A few = দক্েু আয়ে (positive), Few = প্রাে িাই (negative)।

Structure: A few/Few + plural noun

Example: A few initiatives have succeeded in reducing emissions, but few governments act

consistently.

Preposition

1. Preposition + Object

Rule: Preposition-এর পয়র েবেিে noun, pronoun বা noun phrase/object বয়ে।

Structure: Preposition + noun/pronoun/noun phrase

Example: She is interested in music, which broadens cultural awareness.

2. Preposition + Gerund

Rule: Preposition-এর পয়র verb থাক্য়ল কেটি েবেিে ing form (Gerund) িে।

Structure: Preposition + verb(ing)

Example: He is good at solving complex mathematical problems.

3. Prepositional Phrase

Rule: Preposition + Object = Prepositional Phrase.

Structure: Preposition + object → functions as phrase

Rule 1: Present Conditional (First conditional / েতবযুক্ত ভ্দবষ্যৎ)

Rule 3: Past Conditional (Second conditional — অপষ্ট/ক্াল্পদিক্ বতবিাি)

বযাখযা: বতবিায়ি বাস্তয়ব ঘিয়ে িা এিি ক্াল্পদিক্ বা অেম্ভব েয়তব Second conditional বযবিার িে। েয়তব Past Simple,

ফলাফয়ল would/could/might + base verb।

Structure: If + Past Simple, + would / could / might + base verb

Example: If I had more time to practise speaking, I would achieve a band 8 in the IELTS speaking test.

Rule 4: Past Perfect Conditional (Third conditional — অতীয়তর অিুয়োচি/দবক্ল্প ইদতিাে)

বযাখযা: অতীয়ত কযিা দভ্ন্নভ্ায়ব ঘিয়ল ফল বিয়ল কযত—এিি অিুয়োচি বা অপদরেীি পুয়রায়িা েয়তব Third conditional লায়ে।

েয়তব Past Perfect, ফলাফয়ল would/could/might have + past participle।

Structure: If + Past Perfect (had + V3), + would/could/might have + past participle

Example: If she had prepared more thoroughly, she could have achieved a higher overall band score.

Note (Inversion): Had + subject + V3, + ... বযবিার ক্রয়ল If বাি কিওো যাে:

Had I studied harder, I could have passed. ⇄ If I had studied harder, I could have passed.

Rule 5: Tense & Verb Consistency (েয়তবাত্তর বায়ক্য tense-এর োিঞ্জেয)

বযাখযা: েতবযুক্ত বায়ক্য প্রয়তযক্ ক্লজ তার দিজস্ব tense-র দিেি কিয়ি চলয়ব; িুই-িুই ক্লয়জর tense দিলয়ব িা — ফয়ল অথব ভ্ুল

িয়ব। লক্ষ ক্রুি: েতব (if-clause) ও ফল (main clause) এয়ক্-অয়িযর জিয দির্িিষ্ট tense দিযবারণ ক্য়র।

Structure (টিপ): First conditional → (Present → Future), Second → (Past → would+V), Third →

(PastPerfect → would have+V3)

Example (েদিক্ vs ভ্ুল):

Wrong: If you had attend the class regularly, you might have learn more.

Correct: If you had attended the class regularly, you might have learned more.

Rule 6: Auxiliary Verbs & Passive vs Active (েদক্ত-ক্দর/েিােক্ দক্রো ও passive)

বযাখযা: Active ভ্াঙয়ল Subject ক্াজ ক্য়র; Passive-এ object োিয়ি আয়ে এবং be + past participle প্রয়োে িে।

Auxiliary verbs (am/is/are/was/were/have/has/will/should/could) passive tense েিয়িও লায়ে।

Structure (Present/Past Simple):

•

Active: Subject + V(s/es/ed) + Object

•

Passive: Object + am/is/are / was/were + past participle (+ by agent)

Example:

Active: The examiners grade essays carefully.

Passive: Essays are graded carefully by the examiners.

Rule 7: Practice (noun) vs Practise (verb) — British usage

বযাখযা: British English এ practice (noun) এবং practise (verb) পৃথক্; American English-এ উভ্ে কক্ষত্রেই

practice। IELTS (British register)-এ test centre দিটিে ইংয়রদজ কিয়ি চলয়ল ভ্ুল এড়ায়ত টিক্ বজাে রাখুি।

Structure: practice (n) ; practise (v)

Example: I need more practice (n) in writing; therefore, I practise (v) for one hour every day.

Rule 8: Causative Verbs (make, let, help, have, get — causative structures)

বযাখযা: ক্াউয়ক্ কক্ায়িা ক্াজ ক্রায়িা বা ক্দরয়ে কিওোর ক্ািায়িা; verb-গুয়লার পয়র object আয়ে এবং িূল verb-এর ফরি

দিভ্বর ক্য়র causative verb-এর িাইপ-এর উপর।

Structure & দিেি:

•

Make / Let: Subject + make/let + object + base verb

(She made him apologise. / They let the children play.)

•

Help: Subject + help + object + (to) + base verb — to optional.

(He helped me (to) revise for the test.)

•

Have / Get (causative passive result): Subject + have/get + object + past participle

(I had my laptop repaired. / She got her hair coloured.)

Examples:

•

Make: The teacher made the student rewrite the paragraph to improve clarity.

•

Let: The manager let staff take a break during the long shift.

•

Help: My mentor helped me secure an internship.

•

Have/Get: I had my manuscript edited by a professional proofreader.

Tip: Help can take base or to-infinitive: help + object + (to) + V.
