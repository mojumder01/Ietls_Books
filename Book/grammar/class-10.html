Conditional – শর্তযুক্ত

Present Conditional – If + Present Indefinite + Future Indefinite/present Indefinite.

If you come, I will go.

If the students are serious about their IELTS studies, usually they get higher band score.

Past Conditional - If + Past Indefinite + Past Conditional (Past Conditional হবার জন্য subject এর পর

should, could, would, might + verb এর present form হয় ।)

যদি র্ুদি ভাল করর পড়ারশান্া কররর্, র্াহরল ভাল স্কার স্পরর্ ।

If you studied properly, you could achieve better IELTS score.

Past Perfect Conditional - If + Past Perfect + Past Perfect Conditional (Past perfect Conditional হবার জন্য

subject এর পর should have, could have, would have, might have + verb এর past participle form হয় ।)

If you had studied properly, you could have achieved better IELTS score.

Had I been a prime minister of BD, I could have much spent on education.

Had I been a prime minister of BD = If I had been a prime minister of BD

My teacher helped me (get) or to get this job.

Make, Help, Let – এই verb গুল োর পলর ব্যক্তিব্োচক/ ব্স্তুব্োচক যে যকোন

একটো আসল ই - পলরর verb present form হয়.

Robert let me (escape) the prison.

My teacher helped me (get) this job.

Get, Have - এই verb গুল োর পলর ব্যক্তিব্োচক আসল পলরর verb present form হয়.

Get, Have - এই verb গুল োর পলর বস্তুবাচক আসল পররর verb past participle form হয়.

I am sure if I go home now, I will get my mother (feed) her grandchild.

Robert let me (escape) the prison.
