Simple / Band 7

• They believe it should cost less.

• In their opinion, the price is too high.

• They feel it ought to be more affordable.

Stronger / Band 8

• They think the price should be reduced.

• They argue that it’s overpriced.

• They believe it should be priced more reasonably.
