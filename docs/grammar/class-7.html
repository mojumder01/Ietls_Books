Parts of Speech

1. A noun is a naming word.

2. A pronoun is a substitute word.

3. An adjective is a qualifying (situation, number,) word

4. A verb is a doing word

5. An adverb is a modifying word (where, when, how)

6. A preposition is an identifying word

7. A conjunction is a connecting or joining word

8. An interjection is an exclamatory word.
