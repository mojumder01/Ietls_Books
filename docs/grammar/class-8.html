Paraphrasing

IELTS is all about paraphrase.

Reading – question vs passage = paraphrase

Listening – speaker vs question paper = paraphrase

Writing = same question, words, ideas – ভিন্ন িাবে লেখার নামই হবে

Speaking – part 2- part 3- part 1- speaker এর প্রবের শব্দগুবোর ভিন্নতা অথো আপনার কথা েোর সময় – একই জাভতয় শব্দ ো গ্রামার এর পভরেততন আনবেন ।

অর্থ ঠিক রেখে, রকোন বোকযখক ঠিন্নিোখব রেেোখকই Paraphrase বখে। ঠবঠিন্নিোখব Paraphrase কেো যোয়। রযমন, সমোর্থক বো

ঠবপেীতোর্থক শখেে বযবহোে, বোখকয শখেে অবস্থোন পঠেবতথন, বোখকযে গিন পঠেবতথন ইতযোঠি।

1. Using Synonyms

Paraphrasing-এে সবখেখয় common rule হখেো synonym বযবহোে কেো। তখব IELTS-এ সব synonym কোজ কখে নো।

রযমন “kids” শেটি speaking-এ ঠিক আখে, ঠকন্তু writing task 2-এ এটি informal। তোই writing task 2-এ সবসময়

formal বো academic synonym রবখে ঠনখত হখব।

Example

Normal/Given Sentence

Many people think that university education should be free for everyone, regardless of their financial

background.

Paraphrased Sentence

It is widely believed that higher education ought to be provided at no cost to all individuals, irrespective

of their economic circumstances.

Here, the following synonyms are used:

•

Many people think → It is widely believed

•

university education → higher education

Page 2 / 13

•

free → at no cost

•

everyone → all individuals

•

financial background → economic circumstances

•

regardless of → Irrespective of

2. Changing Word Forms

Paraphrasing-এে আখেকটি কোযথকে rule হখেো noun, verb, adjective, adverb এখক অপখেে মখযয পঠেবতথন কেো।

এখত বোকয structure পঠেবর্তিত হয়, ঠকন্তু অর্থ একই র্োখক। IELTS-এ এিোখব ঠেেখে repetition এডোখনো যোয় এবং lexical

resource রকোে বোখড।

Example

Normal/Given Sentence

Technology develops fast and helps students learn better.

Paraphrased Sentence

The rapid development of technology contributes to improved learning among students.

Here, the following word form changes are used:

•

develops (verb) → development (noun)

•

fast (adverb) → rapid (adjective)

•

helps (verb) → contributes to (verb phrase, academic form)

•

learn (verb) → learning (noun)

3. Changing Sentence Structure

Paraphrasing-এে আখেকটি রকৌশে হখেো sentence structure পঠেবতথন কেো। একই যোেণো Active → Passive বো

Simple → Complex বো Clause order পঠেবতথন কখে বেো যোয়। এখত অর্থ ঠিক র্োখক, ঠকন্তু expression ঠিন্ন হয়। IELTS-এ

এিো েুবই কোযথকে, কোেণ examiner variation রিেখত েোন।

Example

Normal/Given Sentence

Teachers give homework to students in order to improve their knowledge.

Paraphrased Sentence

Homework is given to students by teachers so that their knowledge can be improved.

Here, the following structural changes are used:

•

Active voice → Passive voice (Teachers give homework → Homework is given by teachers)

Page 3 / 13

•

in order to improve their knowledge → so that their knowledge can be improved (Simple

purpose clause → Complex clause)

4. Using Different Grammar

Paraphrasing-এে আখেকটি উপোয় হখেো grammar structure পঠেবতথন কেো।

একই যোেণো modal verbs, impersonal form (It is believed that…), বো opinion expressions (In my opinion…)

বযবহোে কখে ঠিন্নিোখব প্রকোশ কেো যোয়। এখত meaning অপঠেবর্তিত রর্খক grammatical variety রযোগ হয়, যো IELTS

Writing ও Speaking-এ বযোন্ড রকোে বোডোয়।

Example

Normal/Given Sentence

People think education is important for success.

Paraphrased Sentence

It is widely believed that education is essential for achieving success.

Here, the following grammar changes are used:

•

People think… → It is widely believed… (personal → impersonal form)

•

is important → is essential (slight grammar shift with stronger formality)

•

for success → for achieving success (noun → gerund phrase for variation)

5. Changing Word Order

Paraphrasing-এে সহজ ঠকন্তু কোযথকে রকৌশে হখেো word order পঠেবতথন কেো। এেোখন একই শে বো phrase বযবহোে

হয়, তখব বোখকয তোখিে অবস্থোন পোখটি ঠিখয় sentence-টিখক নতুন আকোখে উপস্থোপন কেো হয়। IELTS-এ এিো েুব কোযথকে,

ঠবখশষত যেন synonyms কম পোওয়ো যোয়।

Example

Normal/Given Sentence

In the past, few students went to university.

Paraphrased Sentence

Few students went to university in the past.

Here, the following word order changes are used:

•

In the past → sentence-এে শুেু রর্খক → রশখষ সেোখনো হখয়খে।

•

বোখকযে অর্থ অপঠেবর্তিত রর্খকও structure আেোিো হখয় রগখে।

6. Using Definitions / Explanations

Paraphrasing-এে আখেকটি রকৌশে হখেো একটি শেখক define বো explain কেো। যেন exact synonym পোওয়ো যোয়

নো বো বোেবোে একই শে বযবহোে কেখে repetition হয়, তেন শখেে পঠেবখতথ তোে অর্থ/বণথনো বযবহোে কেো যোয়। IELTS

examiner এিোখক lexical range ঠহখসখব গণয কেখবন।

Example

Normal/Given Sentence

Illiteracy is still a major problem in many developing countries.

Paraphrased Sentence

The inability to read and write remains a significant issue in numerous developing nations.

Here, the following definition/explanation is used:

•

Illiteracy → The inability to read and write (direct definition instead of one-word noun)

•

major problem → significant issue (synonym + formal expression)

•

countries → nations (slight synonym shift for variation)

7. Generalization or Specification

Paraphrasing-এে আখেকটি কোযথকে রকৌশে হখেো রকোখনো শেখক সোযোেণ (general) বো ঠনর্িিষ্ট (specific) েূখপ

পঠেবতথন কেো।

•

Generalization = broader শে বযবহোে কেো।

•

Specification = আেও ঠনর্িিষ্ট শে বযবহোে কেো।

IELTS Writing & Speaking-এ বোেবোে একই শে বযবহোে এডোখত এই রকৌশে অখনক কোখজ েোখগ।

Example 1 (Generalization)

Normal/Given Sentence

Facebook has changed the way students communicate.

Paraphrased Sentence

Social media platforms have transformed the way learners interact.

Here, the following generalization is used:

•

Facebook → Social media platforms (specific → general)

•

students → learners (slight synonym shift, still academic)

•

communicate → interact (synonym with broader scope)

Page 5 / 13

Example 2 (Specification)

Normal/Given Sentence

Children enjoy playing games.

Paraphrased Sentence

Youngsters enjoy playing video games such as online computer games and console-based

entertainment.

Here, the following specification is used:

•

games → video games such as online computer games and console-based entertainment

(general → specific detail)

•

children → youngsters (synonym, more academic)

8. Changing Clause Types

Paraphrasing-এে আখেকটি রকৌশে হখেো clause-এে যেন পঠেবতথন কেো।

একই অর্থ েোেখত ঠগখয় আমেো:

•

Because-clause → Due to / Owing to + noun phrase

•

Although/While-clause → However / Despite / In spite of + noun phrase

•

Simple clause → Complex clause

এিোখব পঠেবতথন কেখত পোঠে। IELTS examiner এটি grammatical range ঠহখসখব গণয কেখবন।

Example 1 (Cause → Prepositional phrase)

Normal/Given Sentence

Students use online learning because it is convenient.

Paraphrased Sentence

Owing to its convenience, learners prefer online education.

Here, the following clause change is used:

•

because it is convenient (cause clause) → owing to its convenience (prepositional phrase)

Example 2 (Contrast clause → Linking device)

Normal/Given Sentence

Although education is free, many students drop out.

Paraphrased Sentence

Education is free; however, a large number of learners still leave school.

Here, the following clause change is used:

•

Although education is free… (subordinating clause) → Education is free; however… (two

independent clauses with connector)

9. Combining or Splitting Sentences

এই রকৌশখে িুটি বোকয একত্র কেো হয় (combining) বো একটি বোকযখক রিখে রেেো হয় (splitting)। শে অপঠেবর্তিত রর্খক

structure পঠেবর্তিত হয়।

Example 1 (Combining Sentences)

Normal/Given Sentences

The population increased. The government built more schools.

Paraphrased Sentence

The government built more schools as the population increased.

Example 2 (Splitting Sentences)

Normal/Given Sentence

The internet has advantages and disadvantages that affect education.

Paraphrased Sentences

The internet has advantages that affect education. It also has disadvantages.

10. Using Antonyms

Paraphrasing-এে আখেকটি কোযথকে রকৌশে হখেো antonym বযবহোে কেো।

এখেত্রত্র আমেো বোখকযে positive → negative বো negative → positive েূপ বিেোই। িুইবোে েূপ বিেোখে অর্থ

অপঠেবর্তিত র্োখক, ঠকন্তু expression academic ও ঠিন্ন হয়।

Example

Normal/Given Sentence (Essay Question)

Some people think that children should not be forced to study subjects they do not like.

Paraphrased Sentence

Some people believe that children should be free to choose only the subjects they enjoy studying.

Here, the following antonym change is used:

•

should not be forced → should be free to choose

•

subjects they do not like → subjects they enjoy studying
