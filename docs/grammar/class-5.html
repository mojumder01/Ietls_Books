Verbs

Verbs একটি বাক্কের ইঞ্জিক্ের মক্ া—এগুক্ াই বাক্কের ঞ্জিযা, অবস্থা, বা ঘিোর শঞ্জি ও ঞ্জিক ঞ্জেক্িেশ কক্র মমািকথায

গঞ্জ প্রকৃঞ্জ ঞ্জের্োরণ কক্র। একটি verb মকাক্ো action (কাজ), state (অবস্থা), বা occurrence (ঘিো) প্রকাশ কক্র।

প্রঞ্জ টি complete sentence-এ অন্ত একটি verb থাকা আবশেক।

Verb শুর্ু কাক্জর কথা বক্ ো—এটি tense (সময), aspect (কাক্জর র্রণ/অবস্থা), mood (ভাব), এবং modality

(সম্ভাবো, ইচ্ছা বা বার্েবার্ক া) ঞ্জেক্যও থে মিয।

Usage of Verbs

Rule 1: Singular Subject → Singular Verb

Explanation:

Present simple tense-এ singular subject হক্ verb-এর সাক্থ সার্ারণ -s বা -es ম াগ হয (third person singular

form)।

Structure: Singular Subject + Verb(s/es)

Example:

The professor explains complex theories in a way that inspires her students. She dedicates her

weekends to exploring rare manuscripts.

Why Used:

“Professor” singular, াই explains ও dedicates—িুক্িাক্ ই -s ুি হক্যক্ছ।

Rule 2: Plural Subject → Plural Verb

Explanation:

Plural subject present simple tense-এ verb-এর base form বেবহার কক্র, মকাক্ো -s বা -es ুি হয ো।

Structure: Plural Subject + Base Verb

Example:

The students attend workshops across the city. They share insights and

apply them to real-world projects.

Why Used:

“Students” plural, াই attend, share, apply base form-এ আক্ছ।

Rule 3: Compound Subject with ‘and’ → Plural Verb

Explanation:

িুই বা ক্ াঞ্জর্ক subject “and” ঞ্জিক্য ুি হক্ plural হক্য ায এবং plural verb মেয।

Structure: Subject 1 and Subject 2 + Plural Verb

Example:

Jack and Jill are preparing for the debate competition. They

practice together every evening under the library lights.

Why Used:

“Jack and Jill” িুইজে, াই plural verb are preparing বেবহার হক্যক্ছ।

Rule 4: Compound Subject as One Unit → Singular Verb

Explanation:

খে িুটি noun একসাক্থ একটি র্ারণা, প্রঞ্জ ষ্ঠাে, বা খাবার মবাঝায, খে singular verb মেয।

Structure: Compound Noun (one unit) + Singular Verb

Example:

Macaroni and cheese is served hot at every family gathering. It brings back memories of childhood

dinners.

Why Used:

“Macaroni and cheese” একটি dish, াই singular verb is বেবহার হক্যক্ছ।

Rule 5: Proximity Rule (Or/Nor)

Explanation:

Subject “or” বা “nor” ঞ্জিক্য ুি হক্ verb কাক্ছর subject-এর সাক্থ agree কক্র।

Structure: Subject 1 or/nor Subject 2 + Verb (match with nearer subject)

Example:

Neither the teacher nor the students are ready for the surprise test.

Neither the students nor the teacher is aware of the schedule change.

Why Used:

প্রথম বাক্কে “students” plural → are.

ঞ্জি ীয বাক্কে “teacher” singular → is.

Rule 6: Distributive Pronouns → Singular Verb

Explanation:

Distributive pronouns (Each, Every, Either, Neither, Anyone, Someone, No one, Nobody) সবসময singular

verb মেয।

Structure: Pronoun + Singular Verb

Example:

Each of the finalists is receiving a certificate. Everyone loves a fair contest.

Why Used:

“Each” ও “Everyone” singular, াই is ও loves singular verb।

Rule 7: Plural Indefinite Pronouns → Plural Verb

Explanation:

Plural indefinite pronouns (few, many, several, both) plural verb মেয।

Structure: Pronoun + Plural Verb

Example:

Many have voiced their concerns about the new policy. Several are working to find solutions.

Why Used:

“Many” ও “Several” plural, াই have ও are plural verb।

Rule 8: Uncountable Nouns → Singular Verb

Explanation:

Uncountable nouns (water, furniture, information) singular verb মেয।

Structure: Uncountable Noun + Singular Verb

Example:

Water flows steadily through the mountain stream. Furniture in the old palace is handcrafted.

Why Used:

“Water” ও “Furniture” uncountable, াই flows ও is singular verb।

Rule 9: Collective Nouns → Singular Verb (American English)

Explanation:

AmE-ম collective noun singular verb মেয।

Structure: Collective Noun + Singular Verb

Example:

The committee meets every Friday to review proposals. The team wins regularly in national

tournaments.

Why Used:

“Committee” ও “Team” singular ঞ্জহক্সক্ব র্রা হক্যক্ছ, াই meets, wins singular verb।

Rule 10: Collective Nouns → Plural Verb (British English)

Explanation:

BrE-ম collective noun plural verb মেয।

Structure: Collective Noun + Plural Verb

Example:

The committee are reviewing the environmental report. The team are celebrating their

latest win.

Why Used:

“Committee” ও “Team” plural ঞ্জহক্সক্ব র্রা হক্যক্ছ (BrE usage), াই are reviewing, are celebrating plural verb.

Rule 11: Measurements, Time, Money, Distance → Singular Verb

Explanation:

খে measurement, time, money, বা distance একক (unit) ঞ্জহক্সক্ব র্রা হয, খে singular verb মেয।

Structure: Measurement / Time / Money / Distance + Singular Verb

Example:

Ten years is a long time to master a craft.

Five miles is too far to walk in the heat.

Why Used:

"Ten years" এবং "Five miles" একক ঞ্জহক্সক্ব র্রা হক্যক্ছ, াই is singular verb।

Rule 12: There / Here + Verb + Subject

Explanation:

“There” বা “Here” ঞ্জিক্য বাকে শুরু হক্ verb subject-এর সাক্থ agree কক্র (subject verb-এর পক্র আক্স)।

Structure: There / Here + Verb + Subject

Example:

There is a book on the table.

There are several books on the shelf.

Why Used:

প্রথম বাক্কে “book” singular → is।

ঞ্জি ীয বাক্কে “books” plural → are।

Rule 13: Titles, Names, Countries, Organizations → Singular Verb

Explanation:

ঞ্জিও plural form থাক্ক, proper name singular ঞ্জহক্সক্ব র্রা হয।

Structure: Proper Noun + Singular Verb

Example:

The United States is known for its diverse culture.

The Avengers is a popular movie franchise.

Why Used:

উভয মেত্রেই োম singular র্ারণা মবাঝায, াই singular verb।

Rule 14: Plural Forms with Singular Meanings → Singular Verb

Explanation:

ঞ্জকছু plural-looking nouns আসক্ singular অথে বহে কক্র (ক্ মে: news, mathematics, economics)।

Structure: Noun (singular meaning) + Singular Verb

Example:

The news is shocking.

Mathematics is an essential subject.

Why Used:

“News” ও “Mathematics” singular অথে বহে করক্ছ, াই is singular verb।

Rule 15: Some Nouns Ending in -s are Singular

Explanation:

ঞ্জকছু মরাগ, ঞ্জবষয, বা শাক্ের োম plural-looking হক্ ও singular verb মেয।

Structure: Noun ending in -s (singular meaning) + Singular Verb

Example:

Measles is a contagious disease.

Physics is fascinating to study.

Why Used:

উভযই singular অক্থে বেবহৃ , াই is singular verb।

Rule 16: Subjects with ‘with’, ‘as well as’, ‘along with’

Explanation:

মূ subject-এর সাক্থ extra phrase ুি হক্ ও verb মূ subject-এর সাক্থ agree কক্র।

Structure: Main Subject + (with/as well as/along with ...) + Verb

Example:

The teacher, along with the students, is present in the hall.

Why Used:

মূ subject “teacher” singular → is।

Rule 17: Relative Pronouns (who, which, that)

Relative Pronoun – just আগের শব্দটাগে ব াঝায় ।

Explanation:

Relative pronoun-এর পক্র verb আক্গর noun (antecedent)-এর সাক্থ agree কক্র।

Structure: Antecedent + Relative Pronoun + Verb

Example:

She is one of the students who work hard.

It is I who am responsible.

Why Used:

প্রথম বাক্কে “students” plural → work।

ঞ্জি ীয বাক্কে “I” singular → am।

Rule 18: Don’t vs. Doesn’t

Explanation:

3rd person singular → “doesn’t”

I/You/We/They → “don’t”

Structure: Subject + do/does + not + Base Verb

Example:

He doesn’t know the answer.

I don’t agree with the decision.

Why Used:

“He” → 3rd person singular → doesn’t।

“I” → don’t।

Rule 19: ‘None’, ‘Some’, ‘All’, ‘Most’

Explanation:

Verb noun-এর অক্থের ওপর ঞ্জেভের কক্র—singular হক্ singular verb, plural হক্ plural verb।

Structure: Quantifier + of + Noun + Verb

Example:

Page 10 / 18

None of the books are missing.

None of the money is missing.

Why Used:

“Books” plural → are।

“Money” singular → is।

Rule 20: Irregular Plural Nouns

Explanation:

Irregular plural nouns (children, people, mice) plural verb মেয।

Structure: Irregular Plural Noun + Plural Verb

Example:

Children play in the park.

People have different tastes.

Why Used:

উভযই plural noun → plural verb।

Rule 21: "Half of", "A lot of", "Some of"

Explanation:

Verb object-of-preposition ( া “of” এর পক্র আক্ছ)-এর সাক্থ agree কক্র।

Structure: Quantifier + of + Noun + Verb

Example:

A lot of the cake is gone.

Some of the students are absent.

Why Used:

“Cake” singular → is।

“Students” plural → are।

1. NEVER EVER: Base Verb After am / is / are / was / were

•

Rule: am/is/are/was/were এর পক্র base verb থাকক্ বাকে ভু হয; continuous tense চাইক্ V-ing

াক্গ, passive চাইক্ V3 াক্গ।

•

Wrong: I am eat lunch.

•

Correct: I am eating lunch. (eat → খাওযা)

•

Another Correct: The work was completed yesterday. (completed → মশষ করা)

Why Not:

Am/is/are/was/were → main verb ঞ্জহক্সক্ব ো, auxiliary ঞ্জহক্সক্ব কাজ কক্র, াই এক্ির পক্র মূ verb-এর সঞ্জঠক

form বসাক্ হক্ব।

Passage Example:

Wrong: He is talk to the manager right now.

He is talking to the manager about a potential (সম্ভাব্য) partnership.

2. NEVER EVER: Base Verb After have / has / had

•

Rule: have/has/had এর পক্র perfect tense হক্ V3 (past participle) াক্গ, base verb েয।

•

Wrong: I have go to the meeting already.

•

Correct: I have gone to the meeting already. (gone → ঞ্জগক্যঞ্জছ)

Why Not:

Perfect tense সবসময V3 মেয। Base verb বেবহার করক্ tense structure মভক্ঙ ায।

Passage Example:

Wrong: She had finish her homework before dinner.

She had finished her homework before dinner, demonstrating (প্রদর্শন করা) excellent time

management.

3. NEVER EVER: non-Base Verb After Modal Verbs (can, could, will, would, shall, should, may, might,

must)

•

Rule: modal verb-এর পক্র সবসময base verb আক্স — এখাক্ে double tense হয ো।

•

Wrong: He can goes there tomorrow.

•

Correct: He can go there tomorrow. (go → াওযা)

Why Not:

Modal verbs fixed rule: Modal + base verb (no -s, no -ed, no -ing). Adding tense markers twice breaks

the grammar.

Passage Example:

Wrong: You must completes this task today.

You must complete this task today to meet (পুরণ করা) the deadline.

4. NEVER EVER: Double Past After Did

•

Rule: did = already past, াই এর পক্র base verb বসক্ব, past form েয।

•

Wrong: I didn’t went to the class.

•

Correct: I didn’t go to the class. (go → াওযা)

Why Not:

Double past makes sentence look awkward and grammatically broken.

Passage Example:

Wrong: He didn’t visited the museum last month.

He didn’t visit the museum last month because it was undergoing (চলমান থাকা) renovation.

5. NEVER EVER: Base Verb After Be in Passive Voice Without V3

•

Rule: passive voice এ am/is/are/was/were + V3 বক্স, base verb বা V-ing েয।

•

Wrong: The book was write by an expert.

•

Correct: The book was written by an expert. (written → ম খা হক্যক্ছ)

Why Not:

Passive always requires past participle after be.

Passage Example:

Wrong: The project is manage by an experienced team.

The project is managed by an experienced team with exceptional (অসাধারণ) skill.

6. NEVER EVER: Base Verb After Present Continuous/Perfect Continuous Auxiliaries

•

Rule: am/is/are/was/were + been → continuous হক্ সবসময V-ing াগক্ব।

•

Wrong: I have been work here for two years.

•

Correct: I have been working here for two years. (working → কাজ করঞ্জছ)

Why Not:

Continuous = action in progress → needs V-ing.

Passage Example:

Wrong: They were been prepare for the conference.

They had been preparing for the conference for several weeks to ensure (নননিত করা) success.
