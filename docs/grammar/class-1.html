Person related vocabularies

Positive characteristic

Ambitious (উচ্চাকাঙ্খী)

He has an ambitious mindset, in terms of building a career.

Considerate (সহানুভূতিশীল)

Being considerate is a great virtue.

Cheerful (প্রফুল্ল/আনতিি)

She always has a cheerful smile on her face.

Outgoing (তিশুক)

She's always been very outgoing and she's got heaps of friends.

Supportive (অনযকক সাহায্য করকি আগ্রহী)

Mr. Kamal is very supportive to his family as well as friends.

Physical feature

Height (উচ্চিা): Tall, short or average height.

Being tall is the first thing to notice of her.

Build (গ ন): Chubby, thin, petite, stocky, muscular.

He is of muscular build and he tried hard to get the physique.

Hair (চুল): Bald, straight, curly, wavy.

Idioms & Phrases of character

Broad-minded (িি িকনর অতধকারী)

She has a very broad-minded attitude towards others’ opinions.

Fun-loving (আকিাদ্ তপ্রয়)

The Irish are known for their fun-loving culture.

Fair-mindedness (সিাইকক সিানভাকি চদ্খার ক্ষিিা সম্পন্ন)

If we are to criticize commercial salmon fishermen, we have to be fair-minded.

Painfully shy (অতিতরক্ত লজ্জাধারী)

I was painfully shy and got picked on a lot.

Two-faced (িুকখ এক, অন্তকর আকরক)

I soon learned that he was two-faced.

Thick-skinned (সিাকলাচনা িা তিরস্কাকর অনুভূতিহীন/ গণ্ডাকরর চািিা)

I suppose you have to be pretty thick-skinned to be a politician.

Self-effacing (তনকজকক অকনযর কাকে জাতহর করকি অতনচ্ছুক)

He is so modest and self-effacing which also makes him popular with his peers.
