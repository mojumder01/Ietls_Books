Thesis Statement - Introduction 3rd Sentence

Introduction:

In this day and age, it seems that the young do not show respect

for the elderly in many nations. A closer look reveals that a

range of factors trigger it and has some dire consequences.

Body Para 1: topic+ i1s1

Sadly, a number of forces can be held accountable for it. Firstly, old people are

often conservative in terms of adapting to new era (idea 1). For example, they

Page 6 / 8

believe that stable jobs in state-owned companies are a better choice

rather than encouraging the young to become entrepreneurs (supporting 1). As a

result, young people may find their attitude relatively offensive (idea 1

conclusion). Secondly, many old people do not have much money after their

retirement (idea 2). Consequently, they become their family burden (idea 2

conclusion). Third, children are not taught properly why they should respect their

seniors, so they often pick a quarrel with the elders, such as their grandparents.

Body para 2:

The after path of this trend in one word is gruesome. Firstly, a number of

old people have significant contributions to the society; for instance, Mr.

Abdullah, an 80-year-old man, who is the owner of BBC,

which is a world-renowned biscuit company, has created a

thousand jobs for many young generations for over 37 years. In this

way, it is unfair to say that the elders are useless, and the public morality

standards would decline. Secondly, there are quite a number of

employment positions/roles that are specially designed for old

people- jobs that call more experience than physical power,

hence the qualified elders can contribute more to the society at

present time. Thus, it is a waste of the valuable human resources for

the society if we look down upon the seniors.

In summary, I believe that, people, especially the young, should

respect and learn the wisdom from the elders. By doing this, we

can altogether build a better and happier world.

Sample question:

Many students find it difficult to concentrate or pay attention at

school. What are the reasons? What could be done?

Page 7 / 8

Paying less attention at school for the students is no doubt full of

food for thoughts. A closer look reveals that a range of factors trigger

these difficulties and some steps can be taken to minimize it.

Sadly, a number of forces can be held accountable for it.

Undoubtedly, social media is the root for the disturbance of

learners. If we look around, it is clearly understood that since learners

do not require access permission to social media, learners may waste

their valuable time at any moment or any amount of time. As a result,

the whole system is growing unstable for the students. For instance,

during their examination week, if they pressed their phones around their

studying space, they might not be interested in the book in front of them

at all. Besides, lack of awareness of parents to this matter cannot be

ignored. Parents in general, are always occupied with their personal jobs

and when parents do realize that their children are not heedful of studies,

it is too late. On the top of them, there is a lack of initiatives of the

concerned authority that they cannot make the academic syllabus

enjoyable.

Luckily, to deal with it, a host of measures seem promising. To

start with, no easy situation can be guaranteed to solve the

problem, but conscious effort of parents might be the first step

towards changing it to the right direction. It is also suggested

that persistent effort from the teachers should be made to govern

the increasing problem. It is high time that we, with a bit of help

from the authority, put our best foot forward to begin the end to

the deep-rooted problem.

In conclusion, it is undeniable that many students are facing the

problem of concentrating on time due to aforementioned

Page 8 / 8

reasons and some measures should be taken in order to

build a stronger workforce in the future.
