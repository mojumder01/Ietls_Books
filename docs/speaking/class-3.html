Describe your favourite athlete

Who this person is

How do you know him

Explain why he is famous

Introduction:

I have Plethora of Famous sportsmen in my life such as Shakib Al Hasan, Harshal Gibbs,

Sachin Tendulkar,Shahid Afridi, Chris Gayle and So on. Among them, Shakib Al Hasan who

has already been recognised as a philanthropic and sociable person is one of my most

favourite players in my eyes or at least as good as others.

Who this person is?

He was the ex-captain of Bangladesh National Cricket Team and now playing as a senior

player. He looks really handsome and his height is 5 feet 7 inches, he is a left handed batsman

And slow left arm orthodox bowler. He comes from the South Western part of Bangladesh

named magura.

How do you know him?

To be honest, I know him Since his first ODI match. He scored 57 runs and took 3 wickets.

He was elected man of the match on that day. After finishing the match I Had reckoned that he

would have been the best player in the world and now it has come true. From that time I have

started to follow him on Instagram and Facebook.

Explain why he is famous

Now the question is why do I prefer this person most? First of all, he is not only a hard hitting

batsman but also a great bowler. As per ICC all rounder ranking, he has been ranked number

one several times.

Secondly, this number one all rounder is an inspiring person for almost all the youngsters.

Especially because most of the students get highly motivated from his attitudes, behaviour and

speeches.

Thirdly, he is also renowned for his patriotism as he is the founder of Moscow Cricket Academy

where 300 students are practicing cricket Under the surveillance of Salauddin who is also a

mentor of Shakib Al Hasan. Moreover, he donated half of his financial awards to Charities,

although he himself belongs to a middle class family.

Additional speech (after completing all sub questions)

As far as I could vividly remember, once upon a time I was going to Cox's Bazar from Dhaka by

air. Suddenly I noticed my favourite player Shakib Al Hasan and more fortunately he sat beside

me. We gossiped regarding cricket and so many topics during our whole journey and I

requested for a selfie and spontaneously he accepted that. Never have I experienced such

kinds of remarkable Journeys in my life.
