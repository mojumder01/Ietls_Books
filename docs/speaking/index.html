Q-1. What is your name?

Answer: My name’s ……………………. (Name- English Pronunciation)

Q-2. What can I call you?

Answer: Please call me ……………….

Q-3. Does your name have any special meaning?

Answer: I am afraid; I don’t know the meaning of my name. I will ask my mother regarding this

later.

Q-4. Do you work or study?

Answer: I have completed my bachelor degree in electrical and electronic engineering from

Stamford university Bangladesh. Now I am a full-time employee.

Q-5. Do you think you are a good neighbor?

Answer: It’s really a tricky one. I didn’t think about it before. From my point of view, i have all

the best qualities for being a good neighbor. However, I don’t have any bad relationship with my

nearest kith and kin.

Q-6. Do you like your subject/job?

Answer: Yes, I do love the work I do. I like to be productive in my job field. In fact, my job brings

me my bread and butter and i am grateful for having such a nice job in a prestigious organization.

Q-7. Whom did you talk to on your mobile phone last time?

Answer: Well, about one hour ago I communicated with my mother through mobile. I asked for

pray regarding my exam while I was talking to her. She inspired me for better exam.

Q-8. Do you like movies?

Answer: I am a movie phanatic person. To be sophisticated I always wait for my free time to

enjoy several hindi and English movies. However, my favourite movie is 3 idiots which is not only

inspirational but also virtuous.

Q-9. Where are you from?

Answer: Well, I am from Khulna, which is the south western part of bangladesh. The people of

my city is easy going, hospitable and amicable. However, I have been living in Dhaka for last 5

years.

Q-10. What do you like doing in your free time?

Answer: Well, I love reading, cooking, gardening when i get some free time. Apart from this i like

to watch several hindi and english movies in my leisure time. My favourite movie is 3 idiots.

Q-11. Why are you performing ielts?

Answer: To be honest, ielts has been part and parcel in my academic life. Last time I obtained

6.5 but i require at least 7 to admit my desired university in canada. So I am just waiting for my

intended score.

Q-12. What kind of song do you like most?

Answer: I would like to express my biased opinion that some sort of song perturbs readers mind.

So i hardly hear traditional song. In addition, most of the song with music are prohibited in our

religion. But sometimes i hear isolated vocal song/.

Q-13. Is it important to you to eat healthy food?

Answer: I reckon it’s really crucial to take better/healthy/nutritious food. It’s vital because we can

keep us fit and active by eating healthy food. Moreover, it will keep us free from all sorts of

diseases.

Q-14. Did you enjoy singing when you were younger?

Answer: It’s really an interesting one, I have a load of good memories in my childhood regarding

singing. I have a musical family and my parents always inspired me to sang in my early age.

However, I always enjoy singing in my childhood.

Q-15. How do you decide which clothes to buy?

Answer: It’s really a tricky one, I have different choices of cloths. However, the decision of which

cloths I want to buy pretty depends on the weather. If I choose lighter cloths, then it will use on

summer season and as well warm clothes are for winter.

Q-16. Why is holiday crucial to you?

Answer: Holidays are necessary for rest and relaxation and spend more time with family. In my

eyes people would more active at workplace after their vacation.

Q-17. Describe your favourite journey?

Answer: To be honest, I am a travel lover person, and I always wait for my free time to visit

different places in my country. Actually, I went to cox’s bazar on September 2nd, 2021 with some

of my friends. This was most memorable journey in my life and we enjoyed every bit of times

and took a lot of photos of every possible amazing sites.

Q-18. What kind of pictures do you like having in your home?

Answer: In my point of view, photograph is such a magnificent thing that can make a room looks

beautiful. Moreover, I love to decorate my room with various types of natural scenery that I took

during my several visit.

Q-19. Do you like people taking photos of you?

Answer: To be honest, I do not prefer to capture photograph, especially with less known or

unknown people. I think this is a personal choice and I am not the type of person who likes taking

selfie or self-photograph now and then.

Q-20. What’s your favorite tv programme/show?

Answer: I reckon that TV plays an indispensable part in my free time. I am a tv fanatic person

and I always wait for my free time to enjoy several daily programs. My favourite TV show is big

boss which is presented by indian actor Salman khan.

Q-21. What sorts of food do you like eating most?

Answer: I love homemade foods such as rice, vegetables, noodles, salads and so on. In

restaurants and street foods I like to eat sandwiches, pasta and different types of ice-creams. I

always prefer the quality of food rather the taste. So, I do prefer homemade food to others.

Q-22. Tell me about your best friend at school?

Answer: Well, my best friend in my school days was Toukir Ahmed Real, in fact we are still best

buddy. The best thing in our friendship was we were nearest neighbor also. Now she works as a

teacher.

Q-23. How did your parents choose your name?

Answer: You know my name is Abdullah Al Hossain. I heard that my surname Hossain has been

taken from my father and my mom selected my first name “Abdullah” which meaning is “the slave

of Allah” in this way, they combined my full name. my name inspires me to do more pious activities

specially for the sake of spiritual life.

Or,

Answer: To be honest I cannot say for sure that what was in my parents’ mind when they decided

my name. But once I heard from my father that they thought the meaning of the name was really

good. Who knows they might have been inspired by Indian actress Salman Khan as they named

me Salman.

Q-24. What kinds of emails and texts do you receive about your work or studies?

Answer: To be honest, I receive a few emails from my boss and coworkers every day. Most of

the emails are sent to me for updating me regarding the daily task of any project.

Q-25. What places are there for swimming where you live?

Answer: Well, to be honest, I don’t like to swim as I have a phobia of water. However, a lot of

swimming polls are in my area. Sometimes I went there with my father but I do not have any

interest on this matter.

Q-26. How often do you wear jewelry?

Answer: It’s an interesting one. Nevertheless, I have a load of ornaments given by my parents,

I am not habituated with/accustomed with utilizing those as I believe “Simplicity is the best

policy”. But only for any invitation I wear/put on an eye-catching and state of the earth design

necklace.

Q-27. Is it important to like the people you work/study with?

Answer: To be honest having great colleagues at workplace is really advantageous. I must say

that i have some amazing coworkers besides me who always helps me a lot to complete me

difficult tasks.

Q-28. Do you have a favorite flower or plant?

Answer: Yes, red rose is my favourite flower. In my country red roses are used in marriage and

other ceremonies. It is also a sign of love, romance and new beginning. So i think red roses are

incomparable among all flowers in my eyes.

Q-29. Is summer your favorite time of year?

Answer: Well, my favourite season of the year is summer because of the sunny weather and

vacation. Moreover, in summer lots of seasonal fruits are available such as mango, jackfruit, blue

berry and so on. Among all the fruit mango which is most delicious is one of my most favourite

fruits.

Q-30. Is your name common or unusual in your country?

Answer: Statistically speaking, this is not a very common name. However, from my experience, i

can tell that i have personally met at least 10 others with the same name but with a different

family name.

Q-31. Who normally does the cooking in your home?

Answer: My mother does the cooking in our house and as I have said, she is a magnificent cook.

I often compliment her and inspire her to start a restaurant in the city and she always laughs

hearing this. Sometimes, my elder sister Emma assists my mother in the kitchen. I, on the

contrary, in charge of the grocery and daily shopping.

Q-32. When did you last receive a gift? [What was it?]

Answer: Well, that's an interesting question and it reminds me of a gift that a close friend of mine

gave me a few weeks ago. Marzia went to Italy last November to visit her grandparents and after

she returned, she gave me a perfume made in Italy.

Q-33. When was the last time you laughed?

Answer: It’s really an interesting one, I’m a girl/man who really likes to laugh, so I think the last

time I had a laugh was yesterday. I was watching a talk show last night, and there were some

jokes in it which made me laugh to tears.

Q-34. What kinds of apps do you often use?

Answer: I reckon; technology makes life really easier. As I am a mobile fanatic person and I use

communication apps like Facebook, zoom and WhatsApp which helps me to communicate easily

with my friends and family. I use them on an hourly basis to stay in touch with the world.

Q-35. Which language do you prefer to write in?

I prefer writing in English because these days, majority of written communication is carried out

in English only. In addition, in my occupation and most other professions, English is the most

preferred medium of communication. Hence my most writing work is done in English. However,

when required, I do write in my first and national language also.

Q-36. What was your first mobile phone?

Answer: My first phone was a smart phone which I got in 2008. It was nokia-1650 series given

by my sister. She gave it to me because she upgraded her phone to Samsung Galaxy s3-mini. I

was so happy at that time because I was the only one who didn’t own a phone in our class. It

Q-37. Do you like reading books/movies/song/poem/novel?

Answer: I am a movie fanatic person. To be sophisticated, I always wait for my free time to

enjoy several Hindi or/and English movies. Among them, my favorite movie is 3 idiots which is

not only inspirational but also great virtuous.

Q-38. Do you like song/poem/novel?

Answer: I would like to express my biased opinion that some kinds/sorts of songs perturb the

viewer’s mind. So, I hardly hear traditional songs. Additionally, most of the songs with music

are prohibited in our religion. But sometimes I hear/listen traditional vocal songs.

Q-39. Do you think noise disturbs a person?

Answer: It is certainly true that noise can disturb/bore/annoy people. Noise surely distracts our

minds and doesn’t allow us to concentrate on our work, which leads to irritation. As a result,

noise makes a huge disturbance.

Q-40. Why are birds important in life?

Answer: when I think about birds, the first thing that comes in my mind “some birds look

extreme beautiful”. Moreover, birds are an important creation of nature. Their importance can

only be understood by those who know the importance of nature. They are an important part of

our ecosystem. Apart from that, they eat small insects and reptiles. By doing so, they help in

maintaining healthy and disease-free environment.

10 Personal Questions- Answer Format

Question 1. What is your name?

Answer: My name’s ………………. (English Pronunciation)

Question 2. What can I call you?

Answer: Please call me ……………...

Question 3. Does your name have any special meaning?

Answer: Well, my name’s very special to me as it is to my family. You know my

full name is …………………... The first part of my name, ……………, means

……………. While the second part of my name…………… means ……………….

Therefore, the full/collective meaning of my name …………………

Alternative answer: Well, to be honest, I don’t know whether my name has any

secret/special meaning or not. However, I will allocate a piece of mind on it later.

Question 4. Do you want to change your name?

Answer: It’s a tricky one. Honestly, I didn’t think of it before. However, my name’s

very special to me as it is to my family. I guess I won’t change it.

Question 5. Do you work or study?

Answer: Recently, I’ve completed my ……..HSC/BSC/BBA/BA/Bachelor

degree………. in the Department of ……………. from …………………

University/College which is the largest university in the world regarding the number

of students or, My University/College always ranks no. 1 in my country and I’m

proud of my institution.

Alternative answer: Currently, I’m studying for my HSC/BSC/BBA/BA/Bachelor

degree in the Department of ……………. from …………………

University/College. My University/College always ranks no. 1 in my country and

I’m proud of my institution.

Alternative answer: Currently, I’m working as a/an ……………… at

…………………. which is a private/government institution. My

company/organization is very well reputed throughout the country. I’m proud to be

a part of this company/organization.

Question 6. Do you like your subject/job?

Answer: Actually, I’m quite fond of my subject/job. My study/job focuses on several

interesting aspects of life/nature/science. Apart from that, my classmates/colleagues

are very supportive.

Question 7. Where are you from?

Answer: Well, I’m from ……………. which is a part of the ……………... division

and situated in the ………………. part of Bangladesh. ……………. is one of the

progressive regions in Bangladesh. My area is mostly famous for ………………

The people of my city generally are amicable and industrious.

Question 8. How did your parents choose your name?

Answer: I’m not exactly sure how my name was chosen. However, I heard from

my mother that my ………………. was the one responsible. He/she selected the

name after a considerable amount of thinking and other family members also

supported him/her.

Question 9. Where do you want to see yourself after 10 years in terms

of your career?

Answer: Well, life is full of mystery and it is impossible to say what is in store for a

person. However, in 10 years, I expect to see myself as a/an ………………. But

most importantly, I like myself to be honest and useful to mankind.

Question 10. Tell me about your best friend at school.

Answer: Well, ……………., my beloved school friend, he/she is a/an ……………

in ……………. We used to live in the same village named ……………., which is

located in ……………. We have a lot of childhood memories. In fact, we studied at

the same school, college, and university during the study period.
