AREA – what’s your favorite color? 3 sentences

A – answer- in my eyes, white is the most attractive.

R- reason - Because white is the sign of purity.

E- example - for instance, when I wear any white color dress, I

feel refreshed myself.

A- Answer - therefore, white is incomparable among all

colors.
